Turn Any Website Into a Research Workspace

Paste a website URL and WebRAG crawls it, builds a real retrieval-augmented knowledge base, and lets you ask grounded questions with citations back to the source.

We only crawl publicly accessible pages. Login-protected and restricted content is never accessed.

What WebRAG does

01 — Crawl

Discovers and fetches allowed pages within scope, respecting robots.txt and rate limits.

02 — Index

Extracts clean content, chunks it by section, and generates real embeddings into a vector index.

03 — Research

Ask, compare, summarize, or extract — every answer is grounded with clickable citations.

Supported sources

  • — Public articles and blog posts
  • — Documentation and knowledge-base sites
  • — Product and API documentation
  • — General public web pages

WebRAG will not crawl login-protected pages, private dashboards, CAPTCHA-protected systems, or sites that disallow automated access.

Privacy

What's crawled

Only publicly reachable pages within the scope you configure. No credentials, cookies, or authenticated sessions are ever used.

What's stored

Extracted page content, chunk text, embeddings, and metadata for the workspace you create — nothing else.

Where & how long

Data is stored locally on the server's filesystem/index under your workspace. It persists until you delete the workspace.

External providers

Chunk text is sent to your configured embedding and LLM providers (e.g. OpenAI, Gemini) solely to generate embeddings and grounded answers.

Example research questions

"What authentication method does this site recommend?" "Compare the pricing plans described here." "Extract all API endpoints mentioned." "Summarize the entire site."
